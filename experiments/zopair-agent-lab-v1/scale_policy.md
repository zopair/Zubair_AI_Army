# ZARE Scale Policy

1. No scale-up from a single success.
2. Require 3 comparable verified tasks before promotion.
3. Increase only one variable per experiment.
4. Stop immediately on critical security, compliance, or unexpected spend event.
5. Keep a control workload to detect quality regression.
6. Never use artificial volume, wash trades, self-trading, fake customers, or deceptive promotion as an experiment.
7. Financial transactions remain manual/approval-gated until a separate compliance and security review is completed.
