# ZOPAIR Agentic Sales & Marketing

The sales/marketing layer turns validated products into qualified demand without spam, fake identities, deceptive reviews, or unsolicited bulk messaging.

## Agents

- Market Scout: identifies real customer problems and evidence.
- Content Agent: creates useful technical content from verified product capabilities.
- SEO Agent: maps real search intent to useful pages; no keyword stuffing.
- Lead Qualifier: scores inbound leads against explicit criteria.
- Sales Assistant: drafts personalized replies and proposals for human-approved outbound until compliance gates are proven.
- Lifecycle Agent: measures activation, retention and churn.
- Revenue Analyst: compares acquisition cost, conversion and contribution margin.

## Operating loop

Evidence -> Content/Product Page -> Qualified Lead -> Proposal -> Customer -> Usage -> Revenue -> Retention -> Learning

## Guardrails

- No fabricated testimonials, reviews, identities or case studies.
- No spam or automated mass unsolicited outreach.
- Respect platform terms, robots directives and applicable marketing/privacy laws.
- Agents may draft outreach; autonomous sending requires an explicit approved channel and policy.
- Claims must be traceable to measured product capabilities.
- Marketing budget is subject to the revenue policy engine.

## Product multiplication rule

Do not create products merely to increase catalog size. A product enters the factory only when a real problem, evidence of demand and a plausible unit-economic hypothesis exist. Kill weak products quickly and recycle useful components.
