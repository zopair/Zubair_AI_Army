## English RFQ

Hello,

We are preparing to launch Pink & Red Market, an Egypt-focused women's online store. We are looking for an Egypt-based supplier who can fulfill individual customer orders without requiring us to hold inventory (dropshipping / per-order fulfillment).

Please share:
- Current catalog, exact SKUs/variants, dated wholesale and single-piece prices.
- Minimum order quantity per style/color.
- Whether you accept one-piece fulfillment after we confirm a customer order, with no advance stock purchase.
- Blind shipping / neutral packaging without supplier invoice or supplier retail pricing.
- COD availability, collection fees, payout schedule and deductions.
- Shipping fees and delivery ETA by governorate.
- Failed-delivery, refusal, exchange, damaged-item and return policies, including who pays each cost.
- Permission to use original product photos/videos in our store and ads.
- Stock update process and stable SKU identifiers.
- Legal business name, address and official contact details.

We will begin with a small controlled pilot after all costs and terms are confirmed.

Thank you.

## Decision rules

A supplier is **not approved** merely because it publishes wholesale prices or says it ships nationwide. Approve a pilot only after written confirmation of:
- one-piece, post-order fulfillment with no inventory pre-purchase;
- exact item/variant availability and stable SKU;
- landed cost including shipping, COD and all fees;
- remittance timing and reconciliation;
- failed-delivery, return and damage liability;
- neutral/blind packaging and permission to resell;
- usable product media rights or permission to create original media.

If no supplier accepts zero-stock fulfillment, do not list unavailable stock, collect payment, or promise delivery. Keep the shortlist in research status and continue negotiating.
