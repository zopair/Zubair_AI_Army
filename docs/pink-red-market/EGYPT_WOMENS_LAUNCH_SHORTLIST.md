# Pink & Red Market — Egypt Women's Launch Shortlist

**Status:** Research shortlist only — not approved for Shopify publication or purchasing.  
**Market order:** Egypt first; Saudi Arabia and UAE later.  
**Budget constraint:** EGP 0 initial purchasing budget. Validate suppliers and COD economics before listing.  
**Brand palette:** Red, pink, blush pink, burgundy, white, gold, royal green, black. Preserve this exact palette.

## Selection rules

- Women-focused, visually demonstrable, lightweight, low-breakage, no sizing complexity.
- Prefer products with a public Egyptian supplier price or a credible local demand signal.
- Do not treat marketplace rankings, import values, or third-party estimates as guaranteed sales.
- Exclude cosmetics, skin formulations, supplements, food, medical products, counterfeit branded goods, and fragile products from the first pilot.
- Do not publish a product until supplier stock, exact variant/SKU, landed cost, delivery time, COD fees, return handling, and product quality are confirmed.

## Economics gate — mandatory before listing

For every exact variant, record:

`net_contribution = customer_price - supplier_cost - inbound_shipping - last_mile_shipping_subsidy - COD/payment_fees - expected_return_cost - packaging - discounts`

Do not approve a SKU unless:
- Supplier confirms current stock and exact SKU/variant in writing.
- At least one real product photo/video and a physical quality sample are available for inspection (no sample purchase without owner approval).
- Net contribution remains positive under a conservative return-rate scenario.
- Delivery ETA and failed-delivery/return charges are explicit.
- COD remittance schedule and deductions are explicit.
- Supplier permits resale and provides non-infringing product images or grants permission to create original ones.

## Zero-budget operating path

- First approach suppliers for one-piece fulfillment, consignment, or pay-after-confirmed-order terms. No supplier has yet been verified as offering these terms.
- If no supplier accepts a zero-inventory model, do not advertise unavailable goods or accept customer payment. Continue with organic content and collect non-binding interest only.
- Keep Shopify as catalog/order source of truth. Do not create live listings until supplier and checkout/COD flow are verified.
- Start with 3 hero SKUs (scrunchie, claw clips, stainless bracelet) and 3 complementary SKUs (hair pins, scarf, mini bag) only after the gates above pass.
